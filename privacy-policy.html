Privacy Policy – Parmatma ek

Last updated: 15 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Parmatma ek Android application handles information when you use the app.

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account, the app may collect information that you provide, such as your name, email address, mobile number, language, district, village/city and profile photo.

The app may also store content that you voluntarily submit, such as posts and related images.

2. How We Use Information

Information is used to provide account login, profiles, the Sevak Directory, Margdarshak information, posts and other app features. Information may also be used to maintain, secure and improve the app.

3. Firebase

The app uses Google Firebase services for functions such as authentication and cloud data storage/synchronization. Information submitted through the app may therefore be processed by these services to provide the requested functionality.

4. Advertising

The app may display advertisements using Google AdMob. Advertising services may process information as described in their applicable policies and settings.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by service providers used to operate the app, such as Firebase and advertising services, or where required by law.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information used by the app. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

Information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the app's services and meet applicable legal or operational requirements.

8. Account and Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your account and associated personal information by contacting the developer using the contact details provided on the app's Google Play listing. We will process valid deletion requests subject to applicable legal requirements and legitimate retention needs.

9. Children's Privacy

The app is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact the developer so appropriate action can be taken.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any updated version will be published at this public webpage with a new update date.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or account/data deletion requests, please contact the developer through the contact information provided on the Parmatma ek Google Play listing.
